The product

Everything the node needs, in one crate.

Private AI usually means assembling GPU servers, model serving, a vector database, Arabic retrieval, access control and logging by hand. Sovern Stack arrives with all of it integrated and tested, so the work your team does is connecting data and setting policy, not building a platform.

  • Compute

    A GPU node sized for a department or a site, with NVMe storage and isolated networking, built to be racked in your own facility.

  • Models

    An Arabic first language model with a registry for versions, and the option to run open weight models you already trust.

  • Retrieval

    A vector database, ingestion for PDF and scanned paper, and connectors to the document systems you already run.

  • Platform

    Model serving, GPU scheduling and orchestration, operated as an appliance. Nobody on your side edits a Kubernetes manifest.

  • Governance

    Access by role, retention rules, and an audit trail covering every prompt, every retrieved document and every answer.

  • Support

    Model and platform updates delivered on physical media, health monitoring you can read locally, and an agreed response time.

Sizing

There is no single box. Configurations run from a DG10 class unit small enough to sit beside a desk, through full standalone systems, up to several nodes stacked and networked together when one stops being enough.

We size it with you, against what you need it to read and how many people will use it.

Why isolation matters
Regulated environments restrict where data may be processed. Isolation, residency and outsourcing rules decide what AI you are permitted to run, before model quality enters the discussion.
The strictest case
NCA OTCC-1:2022 requires operational technology networks at critical national infrastructure operators to be isolated from all outside networks. Four domains, 122 sub controls. We design to that bar.
Other frameworks
NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls, SAMA frameworks for financial institutions, and the Personal Data Protection Law all constrain where sensitive data may be processed and by whom.
What we claim

The node is designed for isolation and mapped to the controls that apply to you. We never claim approval or certification by any authority.

Questions

The questions buyers ask first.

If yours is not here, ask it directly and we will answer in writing.

Why not use a sovereign cloud region instead?

A sovereign region solves where data is stored. It does not solve networks that are required to be isolated from every outside network, which is the situation many operational environments are in. When your rules allow a sovereign region, it is a reasonable option and we will say so. When they do not, the node is the way to adopt AI without asking for an exception.

Which models run on it, and can we bring our own?

It ships with an Arabic first open weight model and a registry for versions. You can run other open weight models, and you can pin a version for as long as your change process requires. Swapping a model does not change how governance or retrieval behave.

How do updates arrive if the node has no connectivity?

On physical media, during a window you schedule, with a record of what changed. The node never calls out for updates, licences or telemetry. If you decline an update, it keeps running the version you have.

What happens after delivery?

We stay responsible for the platform: health monitoring, accuracy reviews with your team, updates, and an agreed response time. This is the difference between buying a node and buying a project that ends at handover.

Does it work properly in Arabic?

Arabic is the first language, not a translation layer. Retrieval handles Arabic documents including scanned paper, and answers cite the Arabic source. Mixed Arabic and English corpora are normal and supported.

Who is this for?

Organizations whose rules keep sensitive data inside their own perimeter. Today that is mostly industrial operators under isolation mandates, along with government bodies, financial institutions and healthcare providers with residency and audit obligations.

How does an engagement start?

A briefing, then a short scoping exercise where we map your obligations and your documents to a configuration. A pilot follows on one department and one problem, with success defined before the node arrives.

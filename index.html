The problem

Regulated operators want AI inside their networks. Three walls stop them.

In regulated environments, the rules decide where data may be processed long before anyone compares models. Isolation mandates, residency requirements and outsourcing rules all point the same way: sensitive data stays inside the perimeter. The strictest case is NCA OTCC-1:2022, which requires operational technology networks to be isolated from all outside networks. Not just the public internet. Not just foreign clouds. Every outside network, including sovereign clouds inside Saudi Arabia.

Those rules remove every cloud AI vendor, local or global, from the buying conversation. The organizations still have the mandate to adopt AI. Nobody has built the product that fits inside the rule.

  1. 01

    The regulatory wall

    Isolation and residency rules place sensitive workloads out of reach of cloud AI. OTCC-1:2022 is the sharpest case, with four domains, 122 sub controls and penalties up to SAR 25 million under the NCA Regulations of December 2024. Banking, government and healthcare rules point the same way.

  2. 02

    The integration wall

    Private AI means GPU servers, model serving, vector search, Arabic retrieval, audit logging and observability. Assembled by hand, this takes months and specialist teams the operators do not have.

  3. 03

    The talent wall

    AI infrastructure engineers are scarce in the Kingdom and expensive everywhere. No organization wants to staff a platform team for one department's pilot.

Net effect. AI adoption in Saudi critical infrastructure is stalled at the deployment step, not the ambition step. The budget exists. The mandate exists. The deployable product does not.

Regulatory basis

The rules that keep AI outside your network.

The common principle
Across sectors, the rules decide where data may be processed. Isolation, residency and outsourcing requirements all push sensitive workloads back inside the perimeter, whatever the model on offer.
Industrial and OT
NCA OTCC-1:2022 requires operational technology networks at critical national infrastructure operators to be isolated from all outside networks. Four domains, 122 sub controls. The strictest case we design for.
Enforcement
NCA Regulations published December 2024 (Umm al-Qura) introduce penalties up to SAR 25 million.
Government and enterprise
NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls set baseline requirements for national organizations, including how sensitive systems are hosted and where data is held.
Financial services
SAMA frameworks govern how regulated institutions control third party access and where regulated data may be processed.
Personal data
The Personal Data Protection Law and its implementing regulations restrict transfer of personal data outside Saudi Arabia.
Our claim
Designed for isolation, aligned to the controls above, mapped control by control with evidence your auditor can read. We never claim approval or certification by any authority.
